# GRC / Compliance Analyst

## Overview
A Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) / Compliance Analyst ensures an organization's security program aligns with regulatory requirements, industry frameworks, and internal policy. The role is risk- and process-oriented rather than deeply technical: assessing and documenting risk, mapping controls to frameworks (NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2), preparing for and supporting audits, writing and maintaining security policies, tracking remediation of findings, and communicating risk posture to leadership. GRC is a strong entry point for those with audit, IT, or policy backgrounds and progresses toward risk management, compliance leadership, and CISO-track roles.

## Required Skills
- Knowledge of security and risk frameworks (NIST CSF, NIST 800-53, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2)
- Risk assessment and risk register management
- Controls mapping, gap analysis, and evidence collection
- Audit preparation and support (internal and external/third-party audits)
- Security policy and procedure writing
- Regulatory awareness (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS as applicable)
- Strong documentation, stakeholder communication, and project coordination
- Foundational technical literacy to interpret controls and validate evidence

## Sub-roles / Specializations
- IT/Security Compliance Analyst
- Risk Analyst / Risk Manager
- Internal IT Auditor
- Third-Party / Vendor Risk Analyst
- Privacy Analyst (GDPR/CCPA/HIPAA focus)
- GRC Program Manager

## Learning Roadmap
**Beginner:** Learn core security and risk concepts and the major frameworks (NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2). Earn **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)** for foundational security literacy. Study policy structure and the audit lifecycle.

**Intermediate:** Develop controls mapping, gap analysis, and risk assessment skills. Learn SOC 2 (AICPA Trust Services Criteria) and ISO 27001 implementation in depth. Pursue ISACA credentials such as **CRISC** (risk) or **CISA** (audit); CGRC/CISM are strong options as experience grows.

**Advanced:** Lead enterprise risk programs and multi-framework compliance. Pursue **CISSP** for security management breadth and advanced ISACA certifications (CISM). Build expertise in audit defense, board-level risk reporting, and integrated GRC tooling.

## Notes for Course Generation
Structure modules as: security and risk fundamentals, the major frameworks (NIST CSF, ISO 27001, SOC 2), risk assessment and the risk register, controls mapping and gap analysis, policy writing, and audit preparation and evidence collection. Prerequisites are basic IT and security literacy; no deep technical or coding background is required, making this accessible to audit/policy professionals. Include hands-on lab projects such as building a controls matrix mapping a sample environment to ISO 27001, conducting a mock SOC 2 readiness gap assessment, and drafting a security policy plus a risk register entry with treatment plan.
