# Business Intelligence Analyst

## Overview
A Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst turns raw company data into governed dashboards, reports, and metrics that leadership uses to track performance and make decisions. Day-to-day work involves modeling data for reporting, building semantic layers and KPIs, developing dashboards in BI tools, and partnering with business units to define what "good" looks like. The role emphasizes consistency, trust, and self-serve analytics across the organization — the hardest problems are organizational alignment, not chart-making.

## Required Skills
- **Advanced SQL** — reporting queries, aggregation, performance awareness
- **Dimensional data modeling** — star schema, facts/dimensions, normalization tradeoffs
- **BI platforms** — Power BI, Tableau, Looker
- **Modeling languages** — DAX (Power BI) and LookML (Looker)
- **ELT / transformation** — dbt and data-warehouse concepts
- **KPI definition & metric governance** — one number, one definition
- **Dashboard UX** — visualization best practices and self-serve design
- **Stakeholder management** — requirements gathering, reporting reliability

## Sub-roles / Specializations
- BI Developer (dashboard / semantic-layer build)
- Analytics Engineer (dbt + BI modeling overlap)
- Reporting / KPI Governance Analyst
- Domain BI Analyst (Finance, Sales, Supply Chain)
- BI Architect (platform and semantic-layer design)
- Self-Service Analytics Enablement Lead

## Salary Trend
US (USD): entry ~$75k; mid ~$95k–$120k; senior ~$130k–$175k+. India: typically ₹5–18 LPA depending on city and modeling depth. 2025–26 demand: steady — organizations keep investing in governed self-serve analytics, and BI Analysts who can also model data (semantic layers, dbt) and leverage AI-assisted reporting are increasingly valued over pure dashboard builders.

## Learning Roadmap
**Beginner:** SQL fundamentals; basic statistics; spreadsheet analytics; building first dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
**Intermediate:** Dimensional modeling (facts/dimensions); DAX or LookML; KPI design and definitions; data warehouse basics; connecting BI tools to a cloud warehouse.
**Advanced:** Semantic-layer architecture; governance and metric standardization; performance tuning; embedded/self-serve analytics; AI-assisted BI features.

## Notes for Course Generation
Build the course around delivering a trustworthy executive dashboard: define KPIs with stakeholders → model the data (star schema) → build the semantic layer → design the dashboard → govern and document metrics. Prerequisites are solid SQL and spreadsheet analytics. Stress metric definition and governance ("one number, one definition") over chart aesthetics, and include projects on a dimensional model, a DAX/LookML semantic layer, and a governed capstone dashboard.
